Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2, 2026

On The Way Heating & Air ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our services, or communicate with us via phone, email, or text message.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Fill out a contact form on our website
  • Request a service quote or appointment
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or promotional communications
  • Communicate with us via phone, email, or text message
  • Use our services

This information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (including mobile phone number)
  • Mailing address
  • Service address
  • Postal/ZIP code
  • Service preferences and history

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information about your device, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Access times
  • Pages viewed
  • Referring website addresses

SMS/Text Messaging Terms

Consent to Receive Text Messages

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in to receive text messages from On The Way Heating & Air, you consent to receive recurring automated marketing and informational text messages from us at the mobile number provided. This includes messages about:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Service updates and notifications
  • Promotional offers and discounts
  • Company news and updates

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 10 messages per month depending on your service activity and promotional campaigns.

Opt-Out Instructions

You can opt out of receiving text messages at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any text message you receive from us
  • Calling us at (813) 922-2209
  • Emailing us at [email protected]

After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message. You will no longer receive text messages from us unless you opt back in.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's standard messaging rates will apply to all text messages. We are not responsible for any charges from your mobile carrier.

Supported Carriers

Our text messaging service is supported by most major U.S. carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. However, not all carriers are supported, and service availability may vary.

Help

For help with our text messaging program, reply HELP to any message or contact us at (813) 922-2209.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and complete transactions
  • Schedule and confirm appointments
  • Send you service reminders and updates

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
