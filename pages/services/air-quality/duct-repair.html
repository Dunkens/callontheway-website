Signs You Need Duct Repair

Not sure if your ducts are damaged? Here are the telltale signs:

High Energy Bills

A sudden, unexplained spike in your cooling costs is a major red flag. Leaky or disconnected ducts can be a huge source of energy waste.

Poor Airflow in Certain Rooms

Some rooms are hot and stuffy while others are freezing cold. This often points to a crushed, disconnected, or blocked duct leading to the hot rooms.

Excessive Dust

Leaky return ducts can pull dusty, dirty air from your attic or crawlspace and distribute it throughout your home. It ain't working right if it's making your house dirtier.

Visible Damage

You might see torn, tangled, or crushed sections of flexible ductwork in your attic or crawlspace. Sometimes you can even feel cool air blowing where it shouldn't be.

Deep Dive: Repairing Flexible Ductwork

Most homes in the Tampa area have flexible ductwork, which is prone to being crushed, torn, or chewed on by critters. For tears, we don't just use tape. We use a combination of mastic sealant (a gooey, permanent sealant) and specialized foil tape to create an airtight patch. For crushed sections, we'll replace the damaged portion and ensure the new duct is properly supported so it won't get gunked up or crushed again.

Pro-Tip: When you're in the attic, be very careful where you step. It's incredibly easy to accidentally crush a duct line, which restricts airflow and can cause major problems.

Deep Dive: Fixing Disconnected Ducts

It's common for a duct to come completely disconnected from a vent boot or the main plenum, especially if it wasn't installed correctly. When this happens, all the cool air for that vent is just dumped into the attic. We'll securely re-attach the duct using both mechanical fasteners (zip ties) and a thick layer of mastic sealant to create a strong, airtight, and permanent connection.

Pro-Tip: Periodically take a quick look at your vents. If you see that one has very little air coming out, it could be a sign of a disconnected duct that needs to be addressed.

Why Tampa's Climate Wrecks Ductwork

The intense heat in a Tampa attic is brutal on ductwork. Over time, the high temperatures can make the outer lining of flexible ducts brittle and prone to tearing. Our humid environment also means attics can be damp, and critters like rodents and raccoons often seek shelter there, chewing on ducts and tearing them open. The combination of heat, humidity, and pests makes duct problems a common issue for folks around here.

Check This Before You Call

Before you call, do a quick visual check of the vents in your home. Are any of them closed or blocked by furniture? Sometimes, poor airflow in a room is just a result of a closed damper or a rug covering the vent. Open all your vents and make sure they are clear. If you still have hot rooms, it's a good sign you have a bigger ductwork problem.
