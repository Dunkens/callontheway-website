AC Capacitor Replacement

When a summer thunderstorm rolls through Dade City, the power can flicker, and that’s all it takes to knock out an AC capacitor. It’s one of the most common calls we get. The capacitor is like a battery for your AC’s motors, giving them the jolt they need to start and run. When it fails, your AC unit might hum, but the fan won’t spin, or the compressor won’t kick on. It’s a simple fix, but it can leave your home heating up fast.

We keep our trucks stocked with the right capacitors for all major brands, so we can get your system running again in a single visit. We don’t want you to have to wait. We’ll test the new capacitor to make sure it’s providing the correct voltage and get your air blowing cold before we leave. It’s a straightforward repair that makes a huge difference.

Pro-Tip: If you hear a clicking or humming sound from your outdoor unit but the fan isn’t moving, turn off your AC at the thermostat immediately to prevent further damage to the compressor. Then give us a call.

AC Contactor Replacement

The AC contactor is another small part that can cause big problems. It’s an electrical switch that controls the flow of power to your outdoor unit. Here in Pasco County, with all the critters and insects, we often find that ants, geckos, or other small pests have gotten inside the contactor and caused it to fail. When this happens, your outdoor unit might not turn on at all, even if the indoor unit is running.

Replacing a contactor is a quick job for a trained technician, but it’s dangerous for a homeowner because you’re dealing with high voltage. We can get it swapped out safely and have your system back online right away. We’ll also check for any signs of pest intrusion and give you some simple tips to help keep them out in the future.

Pro-Tip: Keep the area around your outdoor unit clear of debris, tall grass, and bushes. This gives pests fewer places to hide and makes it less likely they’ll find their way into your AC’s electrical components.

Blower Motor Replacement

If your AC system is running but you don’t feel much air coming out of the vents, you might have a problem with the blower motor. This is the fan inside your indoor air handler that pushes the cool air through your ductwork and into your home. Over time, these motors can wear out, especially in a climate where they’re running almost constantly. The dust and pollen around Dade City can also put extra strain on them.

When a blower motor fails, it’s not something you can ignore. We can diagnose the problem and, if needed, replace the motor to get your airflow back to where it should be. We’ll make sure the new motor is the right size and speed for your system, ensuring efficient and quiet operation. It’s a fix that you’ll feel the difference from right away.

Pro-Tip: Change your air filter regularly! A clogged filter makes the blower motor work much harder to pull in air, which can lead to premature failure. It’s the single easiest thing you can do to extend the life of your HVAC system.

Condenser Fan Motor Repair

The fan on your outdoor unit—the condenser fan—has a critical job: it pulls heat out of the refrigerant and releases it into the outside air. If that fan stops spinning, the heat has nowhere to go, and your system can quickly overheat and shut down. We see this a lot in older homes around the historic parts of Dade City, where units have been exposed to the elements for years.

Repairing or replacing a condenser fan motor is a common task for us. We’ll check the motor, the fan blades, and the capacitor to find the exact cause of the problem. A new motor can often get an older unit running like new again, saving you from a much more expensive compressor replacement. We’re all about fixin’ what’s broken, not just selling you a new system.

Pro-Tip: If you look at your outdoor unit and the fan blades are spinning slowly or not at all, but you can hear the compressor running, turn the system off. Running it without the fan can cause serious damage.

Refrigerant Leak Repair

If your AC isn’t blowing as cold as it used to, you might be low on refrigerant. And if you’re low on refrigerant, it means you have a leak. Simply adding more refrigerant without fixing the leak is a temporary fix and against the law. We take the time to find the source of the leak, whether it’s in the evaporator coil, the condenser coil, or the lines connecting them.

Finding and fixing refrigerant leaks can be tricky, but it’s the right way to do it. We use electronic leak detectors and other tools to pinpoint the problem. Once we’ve repaired the leak, we’ll recharge the system to the manufacturer’s specifications. This not only gets your home cool again but also protects the environment and saves you money on future service calls.

Pro-Tip: Look for signs of a refrigerant leak, such as ice forming on the copper lines near your outdoor unit or a hissing sound. These are clear indicators that you should call a professional right away.

Compressor Replacement

The compressor is the heart of your air conditioning system. It’s the pump that circulates refrigerant and does the heavy lifting of cooling your home. When a compressor fails, it’s a major repair. But sometimes, a replacement is a much more cost-effective option than replacing the entire system, especially if the rest of the unit is in good shape.

We’ll give you an honest assessment of your compressor and your overall system. If a replacement makes sense, we have the tools and experience to do the job right. It’s a complex repair that involves recovering the old refrigerant, brazing in the new compressor, and recharging the system. We’ll handle it all and get your AC back to life.

Pro-Tip: A common cause of compressor failure is running the system with a dirty filter or a refrigerant leak. Regular maintenance and addressing small problems early can help you avoid this expensive repair.

Drain Line Cleaning

In our humid Florida climate, your AC produces a lot of condensation. This water is supposed to flow out through a drain line. But over time, that line can get clogged with algae, mold, and sludge, causing the water to back up and potentially flood your home. It’s a messy problem that we see all the time, from the newer homes in Vista Walk to the older houses near the downtown area.

We use specialized tools to clear out clogged drain lines and get the water flowing freely again. We can also install a safety switch that will shut off your AC if the drain line backs up, preventing water damage. Regular drain line maintenance is a simple way to protect your home and your HVAC system.

Pro-Tip: Once a month, pour a cup of distilled vinegar down your AC’s drain line access point. This helps to kill algae and keep the line clear.
