Heating Services

Yes! While Tampa winters are mild compared to the north, temperatures can drop into the 30s and 40s during cold snaps. A properly functioning heating system keeps your family comfortable and protects your home from potential cold-weather damage. We service heat pumps, furnaces, and all heating systems.

A heat pump transfers heat from the outside air into your home and can also cool your home in summer — making it a two-in-one system. A furnace generates heat by burning gas or using electric resistance. In Tampa's climate, heat pumps are typically the most efficient choice since they handle both heating and cooling. We can help you decide which is right for your home.

Warning signs include unusual noises (banging, squealing, rattling), uneven heating, a sudden increase in energy bills, the system cycling on and off frequently, or strange smells when the heater runs. If you notice any of these, schedule a heating repair before the problem gets worse.

Indoor Air Quality

The National Air Duct Cleaners Association recommends air duct cleaning every 3 to 5 years. However, you may need it sooner if you have pets, allergies, recent home renovations, or notice visible dust or mold around your vents. Clean ducts improve air quality and help your HVAC system run more efficiently.

Standard 1-inch filters should be changed every 1 to 3 months. Thicker 4-inch filters can last 6 to 12 months. If you have pets, allergies, or a dusty home, change them more frequently. A dirty filter restricts airflow, makes your system work harder, and increases energy bills. We offer air filter replacement as part of our tune-up services.

Several options can dramatically improve your indoor air: air purifier installation, UV light systems that kill bacteria and mold, HEPA filter upgrades, whole-home dehumidifiers (especially important in Tampa's humidity), and regular duct sealing to prevent contaminants from entering your system.
